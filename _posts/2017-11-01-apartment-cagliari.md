## Cagliari
This project is for the refurbishment of an apartment of 240 sqm in Cagliari, Sardinia, located in an histoical building in the city center. The apartment has got two inner small garden, which provide sunlight from above, without roof. The ceiling is pretty high compared to other italian average apartments, and there are nine large apertures, some with balconies. The client wants to have a design which deal with three different options:
1. one big apartment with large livingroom to accomodate parties with lot of people, a large kitchen, and two big bedroom with bathroom and wardrobe room;
2. the ability to create two additional bedrooms when needed;
3. the possibility to divide the apartment in two smaller flats with a small budget.
