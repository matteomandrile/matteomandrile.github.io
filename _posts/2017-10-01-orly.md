## Floorplans
As per client's requirements, the project propose the subdivision of a larger apartment into two smaller and independent apartments: the first measuring 30 sqm and the second measuring 32 sqm.  
To reduce the cost of the project, and to create environments that adapt to the needs of the inhabitants, the proposed solution limits the construction of fixed walls to the minimum. In fact, in the two apartments the only walls are the ones that define the service spaces, ie the bathroom and the kitchen. This design proposal creates two apartments composed of modular environments that adapt to the needs of tenants. The transition from separate rooms to open-space is possible thanks to a movable partition made of wood frame and polycarbonate sheets. Thanks to the qualities of polycarbonate, this lightweight division allows light to cross the two environments even when divided, while at the same time providing privacy to the occupants.

## Interiors
The proposal comprises three pieces of bespoke furniture: movable partition, kitchen and bed with integrated storage. These can be manufactured in the workshops and quickly mounted on site. Furthermore, these are the same in both apartmentsto keep down the project's costs.

### Movable partition 
This lightweight partition divide the bedroom from the livingroom, and can be occasionally folded, to create a continuous environment. In apartment 1, the movable wall fold itself beside the kitchen, occupying the space of a module (60x60cm). In apartment 2 the mobile wall closes to cover the bed.

### Kitchen 
The kitchen is composed by four 60x60cm modules, plus a pantry with with boiler housing. Appliances are recessed under the kitchen top, like the washing machine, or in a dedicated column, like the fridge- freezer. 

![paris split render4](/assets/img/projects/2017-int-fr-or/2017-int-fr-or-rnd4.svg "paris split render4")

### Bed and integrated storage 
Custom-made furniture made up of a wooden bed frame with integrated wardrobe and storage. This solution help to optimize the space in the bedroom, making the most of the area occupied by the bed.
