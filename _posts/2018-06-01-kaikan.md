## Florence
The project for the new Kaikan in Florence concludes the founding cycle of Soka Gakkai in Italy, and opens a new era of diffusion and development for Kosen Rofu and the human revolution. Nichiren Daishonin wrote the Lotus Sutra as an essential and central text in the practice and diffusion of Buddhism. The Lotus Flower, being simultaneously fruit and flower together, summarizes the universal law of the relationship of cause and effect.  
The circular leaves of the plant that produces the Lotus Flower floating on the pools of water, inspired the iconic and planimetric choice, have therefore determined a precise geometric choice that in the circle, iterated and widespread in the project, expresses other fundamental principles:
- The circle with embodies the value of humanism that in Florence had one of its historical peaks during the Renaissance. The churches with central plants of the Renaissance are inspired by the classical monuments (the Phanteon) and characterized the innovative forms of the Renaissance with the churches of Brunelleschi.
- The project for the Museum in the Historical Villa will complete the Cultural Center with a further strong point of historical testimony and attraction. The three circular rooms are three leaves of the lotus and can symbolize the three great masters, Daisaku Ikeda, Josei Toda and Tsunesaburo Makiguchi.
