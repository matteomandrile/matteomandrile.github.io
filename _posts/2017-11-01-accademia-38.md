## Turin
Interior design for an apartment in the center of Turin. The proposal keeps all internal wall partitions untouched, because the apartment is part of a recent conservative restoration. As a result, the focus shifted towards the design of custom furniture, coupled with the careful selection of other pieces available on the market. The client’s willing to couple classical elements with modern design has been fulfilled by offering bespoke modern furnitures, with simple and elegant lines, and finishes pleasant to the touch, able to withstand the passage of time and modes, paired with furniture items with a vintage touch. Even in the lighting design, this concept is resumed: the lighting designed for the most critical environments is embedded in customized furnishings, and is flanked by hottest and classic lighting fixtures.
